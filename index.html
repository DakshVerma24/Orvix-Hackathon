PG's that are safe and compatible. All on one website



How it works -

1. If a user is looking for a PG, they can find all listed PG's in the area, sorted by rating. They can also see the conditions, including safety ratings, guests allowed, etc.

2. If the user is already staying in a PG and is looking for roommates, they can list their PG, along with other things such as morning/evening person, interests, hobbies, to find the most compatible roommate for them

If the user is the owner of a Pg, they can list their PG along with important safety documents, and other proofs. These images will be verified with AI to prove that they are genuine.
